Research and publications

Research

I work on market design, matching theory and empirical methods. Working on real allocation procedures with municipalities and states generates data that allow their effects to be studied causally. I use administrative data and field experiments from Germany, Hungary and England.

Research areas

School choice

Parents in the lab. All mechanisms are frequently manipulated, by parents about as often as by students. arXiv 2026

Efficiency and strategy-proofness. In Budapest, the Rank-Minimizing mechanism cuts the share of poor placements from 15 to under 3 per cent. GEB 2023

Childcare. Decentralised deferred acceptance for childcare JMID 2021.

Inequality

Shadow education. When children are assigned to the top track, families from the lowest socio-economic tercile increase private tutoring disproportionately. DP 2025

Early tracking. Academic-track schools help children of all backgrounds, but disadvantaged children are less likely to get there. DP 2024

School choice across districts. When districts merge their allocation, everyone gains on average; the gain is equivalent to a school five kilometres closer. GEB 2024

Development economics

Microfinance. In Thailand, more diverse borrower groups improve repayment by ten percentage points. DP 2024

Statistics and data. Work from my OECD years (2014–2017) on the value of official statistics and new data sources.
